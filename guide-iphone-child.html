Set-up guide

Your child's iPhone, set up on their phone

  • Takes about 30 minutes
  • ✓ Checked on iOS 26.1 21 September 2026
  • Version 1.1

You'll set everything up on your child's iPhone and lock it with a code only you know. The restrictions are just as strong as with the other iPhone guide. The only difference is that any changes later also have to be made on your child's phone.

Before you start

You'll need:

  • your child's iPhone, charged
  • a new 4-digit code that your child does not know. This is your "Screen Time passcode".
  • your own Apple Account email and password, or you can create one during set-up, so you can reset the code if you forget it
  • about 25 minutes
Tip: if you or another adult in the family gets an iPhone or iPad later, you can switch to managing your child's phone from it. See the other iPhone guide.

Steps

  1. Turn on Screen Time

    1. On your child's iPhone, open Settings and tap Screen Time.
    2. Tap App & Website Activity, then Turn On.
    3. When asked, choose This is My Child's iPhone.
    Watch this step

  2. Block apps all day with Downtime

    1. Tap Downtime and turn on Scheduled.
    2. Choose Every Day, from 00:00 to 23:59.
    3. Turn on Block at Downtime.
    Watch this step

  3. Choose the apps your child can use

    1. Tap Always Allowed.
    2. Make sure these are allowed: Phone, Messages, Maps, Camera, Photos, Clock, Calculator and your child's school app [TBC].
    3. Remove anything else.
    Watch this step

  4. Stop new apps and setting changes

    1. Tap Content & Privacy Restrictions and turn it on.
    2. In App Store Purchases, set Installing Apps, Deleting Apps and In-app Purchases to Don't Allow.
    3. Under Allow Changes, set Passcode Changes and Account Changes to Don't Allow.
    4. In Web Content, choose Only Approved Websites and add the school website.
    Watch this step

  5. Lock it with your passcode

    1. Go back to Screen Time and tap Lock Screen Time Settings.
    2. Type your new 4-digit code twice. Your child must not see it.
    3. Enter your Apple Account email and password so you can reset the code if you forget it.
    Watch this step

  6. Check it works, together

    1. Try to open a blocked app, like YouTube. You should see a Downtime screen.
    2. Open Messages or Phone. These should work.
    3. Try to change a Screen Time setting. It should ask for your code.
