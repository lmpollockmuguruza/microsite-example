Set-up guide

Your child's Android phone

  • Takes about 30 minutes
  • ✓ Checked on Android 16 and Family Link 14 September 2026
  • Version 1.1

You'll use Google's free Family Link app to choose which apps work on your child's phone. You can install Family Link on your own phone, whether it's an iPhone or Android, and manage your child's phone from there.

Before you start

You'll need:

  • your own phone (iPhone or Android), and your child's Android phone, both charged
  • your own Google Account (a Gmail address), or you can make one during set-up
  • your child's Google Account email and password, if they have one
  • about 30 minutes

Android brands. Family Link looks the same on every phone, but getting to it on the child's phone varies (Samsung, Xiaomi, Motorola…). Options: one guide with brand tips inline (as in step 2), or a "Which brand?" question that swaps in brand-specific steps. The first is easier to keep up to date. The second is easier for parents.

Steps

  1. Install Family Link on your phone

    1. On your phone, install Google Family Link from the App Store or Google Play.
    2. Open it and sign in with your Google Account.
    3. Tap Add a child (or Get started) and follow the instructions.
    Watch this step

  2. Link your child's phone

    1. On your child's phone, open Settings, then Google, then Parental controls.
    2. Choose Child or teen and follow the steps. You'll sign in with your own account to approve it.
    3. When it asks which apps to allow, you can skip. We'll do this in the next step.
    On Samsung phones, Google may be under Settings → Google near the bottom of the list. Help for other brands
    Watch this step

  3. Block all apps apart from the allowed ones

    1. In Family Link on your phone, tap your child's name, then Controls, then App limits.
    2. For each app, tap it and choose Block.
    3. For the allowed apps (Phone, Messages, Maps, Camera, Photos, Clock, Calculator and your child's school app [TBC]), choose Always allowed.
    Watch this step

  4. Stop new apps being installed

    1. In Controls, tap Content restrictions, then Google Play.
    2. Turn on Require approval for all content.
    3. New apps will be blocked unless you approve them.
    Watch this step

  5. Only allow approved websites

    1. In Content restrictions, tap Google Chrome.
    2. Choose Only allow approved sites.
    3. Tap Manage sites and add your child's school website.
    Watch this step

  6. Check it works, together

    1. On your child's phone, try to open a blocked app. You should see a message that it's blocked.
    2. Open Phone and Messages. These should work.
    3. Try to install an app from Google Play. It should ask for your approval.
