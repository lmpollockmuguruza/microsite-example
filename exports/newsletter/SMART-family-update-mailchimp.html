3  Why we’re doing this, and why it’s OK to find it hard
Why it matters
Nobody yet knows the best time for children to get a smartphone, not parents, teachers or scientists. That’s why we’re finding out, with more than 11,000 children across lots of schools, so the answers are fair and reliable. Every family’s part counts.
  
It’s OK to find it tough
Whatever you’ve agreed as a family, it can be hard to stick to when friends do things differently. That’s normal, and you’re not on your own.
