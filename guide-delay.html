Waiting for a first smartphone

What we're asking

Until April 2028, please don't get your child their own smartphone.

That's all. We're not asking you to change anything else. Your child can still:

  • Use a family tablet or computer
  • Borrow your phone
  • Play games and watch TV, as now

Does a basic phone count? Many parents will ask whether a "brick" phone (calls and texts only) is fine for walking to school. The answer needs agreeing with IFF and Cambridge, then stating plainly here. [TBC]

Talking to your child

Children tell us it helps to know why, and to know it's not forever. You could say something like:

"Our school is helping with a big study about children and phones. Our family is one of the ones waiting a bit longer for a phone. It's for one year, and it helps grown-ups work out what's best for children everywhere."

There's also a 3-minute video you can watch together.
