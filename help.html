Ask for an app to be allowed

If your family needs an app that isn't on the allowed list, tell us. Within 5 working days, we'll either add it to the list for everyone or explain why not.

For example, "for football club messages".

Tell us about a problem with a guide

The "tell us which step" links on each guide open this form with the guide and step already filled in. Reports go to the same inbox as the email address, so they can be sorted into "help this family" and "fix the guide".
