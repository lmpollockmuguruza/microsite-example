Welcome to the SMART Study

A research study with primary schools and families, running from April 2027 to April 2028.

This page is the only thing the control group (and the public) can see. It deliberately says who is running the study and nothing about delaying or restricting smartphones, so it can't change control-group behaviour.

  • Does this give control families enough to recognise the study and give informed consent? Or does that information stay in IFF's letters and consent forms?
  • Should the name "SMART" appear anywhere public, if it hints at smartphones?

Look and feel. A study identity of its own: the SMART logo, the Nunito typeface, a warm palette and illustrations, with GOV.UK components underneath for accessible forms. DfE blue anchors it to the department without borrowing GOV.UK branding.

Logos and illustrations: the partner logos are placeholders until each organisation agrees. The illustrations were drawn for this mock-up. For launch, we could keep them, commission an illustrator, or use a licensed set.

Who is running the study

The Department for Education has asked IFF Research, the University of Cambridge and PUBLIC to run this study. IFF Research leads it and runs the surveys.
