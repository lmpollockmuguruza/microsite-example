The SMART Study

A research study with around 80 primary schools in England, running from April 2027 to April 2028.

This page is the only thing the control group (and the public) can see. It deliberately says who is running the study and nothing about delaying or restricting smartphones, so it can't change control-group behaviour.

  • Does this give control families enough to recognise the study and give informed consent? Or does that information stay in IFF's letters and consent forms?
  • Should the name "SMART" appear anywhere public, if it hints at smartphones?

Look and feel. Built with GOV.UK Frontend components in DfE blue, with Arial rather than the GOV.UK font and no crown. A study site that isn't on GOV.UK isn't allowed to use either. That leaves open whether it's built in Webflow or hosted by us, and which languages it's in.

Who is running the study

The Department for Education has asked three organisations to run this study:

  • IFF Research, who lead the study and run the surveys
  • the University of Cambridge
  • PUBLIC

If your child's school is taking part, the school or IFF Research will have sent you a letter about it.
