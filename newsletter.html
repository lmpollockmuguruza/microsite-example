Where's Margot?

A comic to read with your child, aged 8 to 12. One lunchtime, five pupils chase the school dog into the closed-off old wing of Brookfield Primary... and the adventure lasts all year. Every newsletter unlocks 4 new pages.

Draft 3: character sheet for sign-off. The story is planned in full (content/comic-story-bible.md). Chapter 1 will be drawn once the characters are approved. The style is design-first indie: soft shapes, no outlines, a limited risograph palette, and Atkinson Hyperlegible for text. The sheet is also a Figma file: exports/figma/wheres-margot-character-sheet.svg.

Meet the explorers

Rules of the game

  1. This story lasts 12 months, just like the study.
  2. Every newsletter unlocks 4 new pages. Ask your grown-up!
  3. Find Margot's paw print hidden on every page.
  4. Keep an eye on Maisie's map. You might spot the way out before they do.
