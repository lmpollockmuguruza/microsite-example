# Comic story bible (draft 2)

**Title:** *Where's Margot?*

**Format:** an opener ("rules of the game") plus 12 issues of 4 pages each, 48 pages in all. One issue goes out with each monthly newsletter, from May 2027 to April 2028.
**Readers:** children aged 8 to 12, ideally reading with a parent.
**Pitch:** *The Goonies* meets *Indiana Jones*, set inside a UK primary school over one lunch break that feels like a whole year.

---

## The big idea

One April lunchtime, five children chase the school dog into the closed-off old wing of Brookfield Primary. They get lost, find a hand-drawn map from 1977, dodge the strictest teacher in school, and follow the map to a buried time capsule. Because they're stuck inside for so long, they become convinced months are passing, so they celebrate summer, Halloween, Bonfire Night, Christmas and New Year inside the school. Each one lands in the same month as the real newsletter. In February the bell rings, and they climb out into the playground to find... it's 1:10pm. The whole adventure was one lunch break.

**Why it works for the study:** it isn't about phones. It's about curiosity, real-world adventure, friendship across year groups, and the fun of finding things out. No phones appear, because the story happens in school. That makes it work for restrict and delay families alike. It never preaches.

| **Margot** | School dog | Balls, crisps, chaos | Golden cockapoo with a red "Reading Dog" bandana | Starts everything, goes missing, and finds the final clue |

Supporting characters:
- **Mrs Hargreaves**, deputy head: firm about rules, but kind; never a villain. The children hide from her to avoid being sent back to class, not because she's scary. Twist: she was one of the 1977 children who buried the time capsule.
- **Mr Bones**, the science lab skeleton, wearing a Christmas hat by Issue 8.

**Spot the paw print:** a tiny paw print hides on every page (it replaces Bit's pixel). This is the reading-together game.

## Tone and rules

- **Funny first.** One or two jokes per page. The meme lines ("+1000 aura", "it's giving", "6-7") are kept in a list that's easy to swap, because slang dates fast. Check them shortly before each issue goes out.
- **UK details:** Greggs, rain, *Bake Off*-style commentary, Panini stickers, Wimbledon, Bonfire Night, nativity plays, World Book Day, Red Nose Day.
- **Safe adventure.** The pool is drained. The old wing is "closed for repairs", not dangerous. The window is on the ground floor. Nobody gets hurt. Adults are kind but firm, and the story shows them being told: "next time, ask".
- **Inclusive without making it the point.** Maisie's wheelchair is part of the action (sprint, ramp), not her story; her story is her art. Priya's BSL helps the whole group.
- **No screens.** Phones never appear, and the story never mentions them. Being curious and getting out into the world is the message, shown, not told.
- **Every issue:** 4 pages, a "learn" moment, a joke, the map (from Issue 3), the hidden paw print, and a cliffhanger.

## Opener: rules of the game (sent before Issue 1)

1. This story lasts 12 months, just like the study.
2. Every newsletter unlocks 4 new pages. Ask your grown-up!
3. Find Margot's paw print hidden on every page.
4. Keep an eye on Maisie's map. You might spot the way out before they do.
