Which apps are allowed?

Everything is blocked, apart from a short list of apps that most families told us they need:

  • Phone
  • Messages
  • Maps
  • Camera
  • Photos
  • Clock
  • Calculator
  • School app [TBC]
  • WhatsApp? [TBC]

Anything your child installs later is blocked automatically. If your family needs another app, ask us to add it. We'll reply within 5 working days.

The allowed-apps list appears in every guide. It should be stored once and shown everywhere, so a change (for example, a new app agreed on 1 March) updates every guide, the PDF and this page together.
