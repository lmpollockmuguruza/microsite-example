If your child has a smartphone

Please set it up so it only has a short list of apps and websites. It takes about 30 to 40 minutes. There's one guide for iPhones and one for Android phones. Your own phone can be anything. If some of it is already set up, each guide shows you where to start.

Which apps are allowed?

Everything is blocked apart from a short list of apps families told us they need, such as calls, messages and maps, and a short list of websites. You'll see the list in your guide, at the step where you allow them back. Anything your child installs later is blocked automatically.

If your family needs another app, ask us to add it. We'll reply within 5 working days.
